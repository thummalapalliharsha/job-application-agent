Job Description

We are looking for a Junior Generative AI Engineer to join our AI engineering team. The candidate will work on building and evaluating AI-powered applications using Python, large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, embeddings, and vector databases.

Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipelines for knowledge-based applications.
Work with embeddings, semantic search, and vector databases.
Develop applications that interact with Large Language Models (LLMs).
Build natural-language interfaces for querying structured data.
Perform data preprocessing, cleaning, and exploratory data analysis.
Evaluate application outputs and improve retrieval and response quality.
Work with Git and standard software development practices.
Collaborate with the engineering team to develop and test AI features.
